A Google Lens-style tool that extracts text from images using Optical Character Recognition (OCR). Upload any image containing text, and the scanner will process it to extract readable text.

Perfect for digitizing documents, extracting text from screenshots, or translating text from images. Works entirely in your browser—no data is sent to external servers.
