How to Play

1

Move the Claw

Use arrow keys or buttons to position the claw above a prize

2

Lower the Claw

Press SPACE or the "Claw" button to lower it

3

Grab & Win

If successful, the claw grabs the prize and brings it to you!

4

Collect Prizes

Won prizes appear in your collection cabinet

Pro Tip: Smaller prizes are harder to grab but worth more points! Try to aim for the edges of prizes.
