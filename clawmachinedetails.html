Kawaii Claw Machine

A fully interactive claw machine game built with HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript, featuring realistic 2-prong mechanical claw physics, adorable cat plushies, and a kawaii aesthetic design. Experience authentic arcade gameplay right in your browser!

With advanced grabbing mechanics, weight-based physics, and mobile touch support, this game brings the thrill of winning plushies with realistic claw movements and prize-lifting animations.
